## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **end-to-end Business Intelligence and Operations Analytics solution** designed to transform raw Blinkit business data into actionable insights.

The analysis integrates multiple business domains including:

* 📈 Sales
* 👥 Customer behaviour
* 📦 Inventory
* 🚚 Delivery operations
* 📢 Marketing performance
* ⭐ Customer feedback
* 📊 Executive-level KPI monitoring

The project combines **SQL, Python, Excel and Power BI** to perform data preparation, exploratory analysis, KPI development, business analysis and interactive visualization.

> **Goal:** Transform raw operational data into meaningful business intelligence that can support better decision-making across sales, customers, inventory, delivery and marketing.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The project focuses on answering important business questions such as:

* How is overall revenue and order performance trending?
* Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
* Which customer segments contribute the most business value?
* What are the major customer feedback and sentiment patterns?
* How efficiently is inventory being managed?
* How much stock is being damaged?
* What percentage of deliveries are delayed?
* Which payment methods and periods influence delivery performance?
* Which marketing channels generate the strongest returns?
* Which customer audiences and campaigns perform better?
* Where are the major opportunities for operational improvement?

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 👥 Customer

* Regular customers contribute significantly to overall revenue.
* Customer segmentation can help identify opportunities for retention and premium-user engagement.
* Customer feedback and sentiment provide additional indicators of customer experience.

### 📈 Sales

* Essential product categories generate consistent sales.
* Product- and category-level analysis helps identify major revenue contributors.
* Weekday and payment-method analysis provides additional context for purchasing behaviour.

### 📦 Inventory

* Damaged stock represents an important area for operational improvement.
* Product and category-level damage analysis can help prioritize inventory controls.
* Inventory loss analysis provides opportunities to improve stock handling and replenishment.

### 🚚 Delivery

* Delivery performance is a significant operational KPI.
* Approximately **69.2% of orders were delivered on time**.
* Delayed orders can be further investigated through monthly, payment-method and delay-reason analysis.

### 📢 Marketing

* Marketing channels produce different levels of return.
* ROAS provides a useful measure for evaluating marketing efficiency.
* Audience and campaign-level analysis can support more targeted budget allocation.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. 👥 Improve Customer Retention

Develop loyalty initiatives for regular and premium customers and monitor segment-level purchasing behaviour.

### 2. 📦 Optimize Inventory

Prioritize high-damage products and categories and improve inventory monitoring to reduce avoidable stock losses.

### 3. 🚚 Improve Delivery Efficiency

Analyse delayed orders by month, distance, payment method and operational conditions to identify delivery bottlenecks.

### 4. 📢 Optimize Marketing Spend

Compare ROAS, conversion rate, revenue and spend across channels and campaigns before allocating additional budget.

### 5. 📊 Strengthen KPI Monitoring

Use the executive dashboard to continuously monitor revenue, orders, customers, delivery performance and customer experience.
